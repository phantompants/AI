# JP226 Video to Shorts Planner

Created by JP226Prints.

Turns one long video into a week of seven different vertical Shorts, with a Monday to Sunday publishing plan. It works through twelve steps in order, from a timestamped video map to hooks, a cut map, captions, framing, visuals, audio, a rough-cut review and the final copy for each post.

It plans and writes. You do the cutting in your own editor, such as CapCut.

## What you get

1. A searchable video map with timestamps, topics, takeaways and strong quotes.
2. Up to 12 ranked clip candidates, each with a permanent ID.
3. Seven Shorts, each with a different main takeaway.
4. Three on-screen hooks per Short and the best spoken opening already in the footage.
5. A cut map with source and new-Short timings.
6. SRT caption text.
7. A 9:16 framing plan.
8. Up to four visual additions per clip.
9. An audio fix and music plan.
10. A review of your uploaded rough cut, with confirmed and uncertain fixes kept apart.
11. Three titles, three cover-text options and one caption.
12. A Monday to Sunday plan with filenames and open fixes.

## Honest by design

- It never invents dialogue. Every quote has a source timestamp.
- It does not describe footage it has not checked.
- If it can only see a transcript, it says so and turns visual and audio notes into checks for you to run.
- Timing is provisional until you check it in your editor. It does not claim frame accuracy.
- New recordings and things you must create are labelled and kept apart from your original footage.
- No fake urgency, no viral promises, no made-up results.
- Posting times come only from analytics you supply.

## Install

Add the `.skill` file to Claude as a skill in your own workspace.

## Usage

```
> /jp226-video-to-shorts-planner here is my 40 minute video and transcript
> use jp226-video-to-shorts-planner to find seven Shorts for beginner podcasters
> start at step 5 for clip C03, here is my video map
```

Claude first asks a short set of questions: audience, the problem they face, your niche, your goal, the platform, clip length, editor, music preference and a short brand code for filenames. It then runs one step at a time and asks before moving on.

## Origin

The shape of this workflow was inspired by a 12-step video-to-Shorts approach shared publicly on Facebook. All the wording in this skill is original.

## Files
