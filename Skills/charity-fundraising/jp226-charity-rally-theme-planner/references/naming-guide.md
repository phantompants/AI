# Naming guide

## A good team name

- Short: two to four words is easiest.
- Easy to say aloud and spell after hearing it once.
- Hints at the theme or the car.
- Fun without making fun of anyone.
- Does not suggest it is the charity or an official partner.
- Not too close to another team, brand or famous title.

## Methods

| Method | Example shape |
|---|---|
| Pun on the theme | a well-known phrase with one word swapped |
| Alliteration | two words starting with the same sound |
| Role plus theme | "[Theme] Rescue Crew" |
| The car's nickname | the vehicle as a character |
| Place plus theme | the start or finish town plus the theme |

Generate 15 names, cut to 5, then test with the handle check.

## Handles

- Handles are lower case letters and numbers. The script shows the handle each name becomes.
- Try the same handle on every platform so people can find the team. If it is taken, add a short word such as "team" or "rally" at the end, not a random number.
- Keep a short form for X (15 characters).
- Do not copy another account's handle with one letter changed.

## Domains

- `.au` and `.com.au` need an Australian presence check. A registrar will say what is needed.
- Cheaper and shorter is better. Avoid hyphens.
- Register the domain before announcing the name.

## Check by hand

1. Search each platform for the handle.
2. Search the web for the full name in quotes.
3. Search the event's past teams list.
4. Say it aloud to someone who has not seen it.
