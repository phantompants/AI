# Message rules

## Money

- The only place money is given is the charity's own donation page.
- Show the link in full. Check it opens, on a phone.
- A figure needs a date: "$4,200 raised as at 9 October". Check it against the page on the day you send.
- Do not round up. Do not predict ("we'll hit $10,000 by Friday").
- A target is fine if the user set it: "Our goal is $5,000."

## Safe and unsafe wording

| Say | Avoid |
|---|---|
| "Every bit helps." | "100% goes to the kids." (unless the charity says so) |
| "Donations go straight to Variety through their page." | "Send it to us and we'll pass it on." |
| "Our team is raising money for Variety." | "We are the official Variety team." (unless true) |
| "Donations may be tax deductible, check with the charity." | "Tax deductible!" |
| "The event runs 12 to 20 September." | "Last chance to give!" |
| "Thanks to everyone who gave this week." | Naming a donor who has not agreed |

## People and photos

- Ask before posting photos of identifiable people, especially children.
- Do not post a sponsor's logo or name without their agreement.
- Do not share a donor's amount without their agreement.

## Channels

- Keep each message short enough to read on a phone.
- Use one or two hashtags on X and a few relevant ones on Instagram.
- Text messages go only to people who know you.
- Email: a plain subject line that says what it is.

## After the event

Post the final total with its date and say thank you. Keep the donation link live as long as the charity's page is open.
