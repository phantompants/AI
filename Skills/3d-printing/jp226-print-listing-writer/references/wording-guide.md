# Wording guide

Australian spelling, plain English, short sentences. No em dashes. No pressure tactics.

## Structure of a description

1. One line: what it is and what it is for.
2. Key facts as a short list: size, material, colours, weight.
3. What is included.
4. Care and limits.
5. Postage and dispatch.
6. A fair note: "3D printed in PLA. Fine layer lines are normal."

## Safe phrases

| Say | Instead of |
|---|---|
| "Printed in PETG, suited to outdoor use" | "Waterproof and unbreakable" |
| "For decoration. Not for food use." | "Food safe" |
| "Keep below 50°C. PLA softens in a hot car." | "Heat resistant" |
| "Made to order. Dispatched in 3 to 5 days." | "Limited stock, hurry!" |
| "Dinosaur themed desk organiser" | "Jurassic Park desk organiser" (unless licensed) |
| "Each piece is printed in our home workshop." | "Handmade" (unless it is) |

## Keywords

Think about what a buyer would type: the object, the use, the person or occasion, the material. Use real search wording, not made-up phrases. Do not repeat one phrase over and over.

## Example (Etsy, short form)

Title: Dinosaur Desk Organiser, 3D Printed Pen Holder, Green PLA, Gift for Kids Room or Office

Tags: desk organiser, pen holder, dinosaur gift, 3d printed, kids desk, office decor, green decor, dino lover, stationery, pencil cup, nursery decor, teacher gift, desk tidy

Description opener: A dinosaur themed pen holder for desks and shelves. Printed in green PLA. 90 mm wide, 110 mm tall.

## Charity wording (only if confirmed)

Use exactly what the seller confirms, for example: "The seller donates 50% of profits from this shop to Variety - the Children's Charity." Do not add "tax deductible", "100%" or any claim about how the money is used.
